Atlas Design System

Atlas Design System

Industry

Enterprise

Platform

Web & Dashboard

Role

Design Systems, UI

Year

2023

Atlas is a design system I built for an enterprise software team of six designers and eighteen engineers who had spent two years accumulating inconsistent components without a shared source of truth.

The work was less about visual style and more about vocabulary — giving designers and engineers a shared language for spacing, state, and hierarchy so a conversation about a button didn’t take twenty minutes to have.

Rolling it out was as much a change-management project as a design one: adoption only happened once the system made people’s work faster, not just more consistent.
