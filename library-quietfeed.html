Quietfeed

Quietfeed

Industry

Community, AI

Platform

Concept

Role

Research, UX

Year

2026

A concept exploring an alternative to the engagement-maximizing social feed — what would a feed look like if it optimized for a reader’s sense of calm instead of their time-on-app?

The prototype ranks content by a simple signal: would the reader be glad they saw this a week from now? It intentionally ignores recency and virality as ranking factors, testing whether a slower feed can still feel worth opening.

Concept-stage only, built to pressure-test an idea rather than to ship. Some of its thinking has quietly influenced how the Tryblie digest was designed.
