Classroom Copilot

Classroom Copilot

Industry

EdTech, AI

Platform

Web

Role

Research, Strategy

Year

2026

An early-stage concept exploring how an AI teaching assistant could help instructors prepare feedback for design portfolio reviews — born directly out of my own teaching work at Perxels and Cirvee.

The prototype focuses on a narrow, unglamorous problem: giving thoughtful, specific written feedback to thirty students takes hours a working instructor rarely has. Rather than automating the feedback itself, the tool drafts a structured starting point an instructor edits and personalizes, keeping the human judgment central.

Still an active exploration — not yet a shipped product, but one I keep returning to between other work.
