Portfolio Review Kit

Industry

Platform

Teaching Tool

Role

Strategy

Year

2024

A structured framework I built for running portfolio reviews with the designers I mentor — a simple set of prompts and a scoring rubric that keeps feedback specific and actionable instead of a vague "looks good."

What started as a personal checklist for my own sessions has since been adopted by two other instructors at Zebra Africa, with light adjustments for their cohorts.

It’s intentionally low-tech — a shared document, not a product — because the goal was better conversations, not another tool to maintain.
