Zplit: simplifying group plans & payments

Zplit mobile application — group plans, contribution tracking & shared payment flows

Industry

FinTech / Social Finance

Platform

Mobile (iOS & Android)

Role

Product Designer (UI/UX)

Timeline

2024–Present

Overview

Zplit is a mobile platform designed to make group activities, shared payments, and contributions effortless. Whether planning a trip, hosting an event, buying a group gift, or splitting shared subscriptions, Zplit organizes, tracks, and splits costs seamlessly.

Zplit takes away the awkward social stress of asking "Who has paid?" or "When is the deadline?" — allowing groups to focus on enjoying the moment while the platform handles the details.

The Challenge

Group payments are inherently socially awkward and logistically chaotic. Organizers end up fronting large sums of money, chasing friends over multiple chat apps, and manually tracking receipts on spreadsheets. Existing split apps only calculate debt after the money is spent; Zplit was built to handle pre-planning, live collection, and post-event settlement all in one place.

Key Product Features

  • Create Group Plans & Easy Invites: Organize events or shared goals and invite friends via contacts, shareable links, or dynamic QR codes.
  • Flexible Amounts & Deadlines: Set custom or equal contribution limits and explicit payment deadlines for each participant.
  • Real-Time Contribution Tracker: Live status dashboard showing who has paid, who is pending, and total funds collected.
  • In-Group Communication: Built-in messaging to keep everyone aligned on details without switching apps.
  • Activity Logs & Automated Reminders: Transparent transaction logs and gentle, automated notifications that remove manual nagging.

Lessons Learned

  • Social context shapes financial UX: Group financial tools must solve for interpersonal dynamics, not just numeric division.
  • Automated transparency builds trust: When payment status is visible to the entire group, accountability increases naturally without manual intervention.
  • Shareable links drive viral acquisition: Allowing non-app users to contribute via web links dramatically lowers onboarding friction.
