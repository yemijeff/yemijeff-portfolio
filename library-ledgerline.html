Ledgerline

Ledgerline

Industry

FinTech

Platform

Dashboard

Role

UI, Design Systems

Year

2022

An earlier exploration of the same reconciliation problem that later became Payvice — a smaller freelance engagement for a fintech startup that needed a lightweight internal dashboard before it needed a full product redesign.

Ledgerline focused narrowly on one view: a daily settlement summary that flagged mismatches between expected and actual payouts. Many of the ideas that shaped Payvice’s later "what changed" home screen were first tested here, at a much smaller scale.

A useful reminder that some of the clearest design thinking happens on the smallest engagements, not the flagship ones.
